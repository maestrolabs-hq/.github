# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and be kind. Criticise work, not people. Assume the person
you are replying to is competent and acting in good faith, and say what you
mean plainly enough that they do not have to guess.

## What is expected

- Technical disagreement is welcome and expected. Disagree with the argument.
- Say when you are unsure. Confident wrongness costs more than an open question.
- Accept a decision once it is made and recorded, or reopen it with new
  evidence. Re-litigating settled decisions without new information wastes the
  time of everyone who already read the ADR.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, or sustained disruption. Publishing someone's
private information. Deliberately wasting reviewers' time.

## Reporting

Raise it privately with the maintainer: francois.ldaigneault@gmail.com.
Reports are read by one person and are not shared further without your
agreement.

Enforcement is proportionate and, in a project this size, mostly consists of
a direct conversation. Repeated or severe conduct ends in a block.
